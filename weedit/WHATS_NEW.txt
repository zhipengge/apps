首次发布：一篇稿写成之后，按公众号、小红书、知乎、微博各自的规则导出成可以直接粘贴的 HTML 或纯文本。四个平台是四套独立的渲染规则，标题上限、正文上限、图片张数、吃不吃表格和并排图都不一样，超限在复制之前就提示，不用等粘到后台才发现被截断。插入的图片会复制进稿件自己的目录，导出时嵌进 HTML 一起带走，以避开平台对外链图片的拦截。文稿与媒体只存在本机沙盒容器里，一篇稿一个目录，整个目录拷走即可备份或换机器。没有账号，没有自建服务器，不收集数据，不登录你的平台账号，也不代你发布。联网只有三处：取文稿里已有的远程图片、把平台创作页交给浏览器打开、把编辑器里点到的链接交给系统。需要 macOS 14.0 或更高版本。

First release: write one draft, then export it as pastable HTML or plain text for WeChat Official Accounts, Xiaohongshu, Zhihu and Weibo. The four platforms have four separate rule sets, differing in title limits, body limits, image counts and whether tables or side-by-side image rows survive, and any limit you exceed is flagged before you copy rather than after the paste. Inserted images are copied into the draft's own folder and inlined into the exported HTML so platform hotlink blocking does not break them. Drafts and media stay in the local sandbox container, one folder per draft, and copying that folder is the whole backup and migration story. No account, no backend of ours, no data collection, no sign-in to your platform accounts and no posting on your behalf. The network is used in three places only: fetching remote images already in a draft, handing a platform compose page to the browser, and handing a link you click in the editor to the system. Requires macOS 14.0 or later.
