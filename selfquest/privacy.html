自律勇者 · 隐私政策

SelfQuest — Privacy Policy · 最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-09

中文

自律勇者不收集任何数据。 没有账号，没有服务器，App 也不发起任何网络请求。你的习惯、打卡、专注记录只写在这台设备的沙盒里。

我们处理什么、存在哪

数据用途位置
习惯的名称、图标、颜色、重复方式展示今日待办设备沙盒 Application Support
打卡记录（日期、当次获得的经验与金币）连击计算、统计、热力图设备沙盒
专注记录（任务名、时长、是否完成）专注统计与柱状图设备沙盒
角色状态（经验、金币、已购装备、已解锁成就）等级与装备展示设备沙盒
偏好设置（默认专注时长等）恢复上次的使用状态设备沙盒

以上数据全部在本机读写，不会离开设备。

网络

App 不发起任何网络请求。 飞行模式下全部功能可用。

我们不做的事

  • 不要求注册或登录
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、位置、设备标识符
  • 不接入广告、分析或追踪 SDK
  • 不读取通讯录、麦克风、摄像头、健康数据
  • 不把你的数据共享给任何第三方

你对自己的数据有完全控制

设置页提供两种导出：JSON 是完整存档，换手机时导入即可恢复；CSV 是打卡明细，可用 Excel 打开。分享功能完全由你主动触发，内容通过系统分享面板交给你选择的目标应用。

数据删除

删除 App 即可清除全部数据。也可以在 App 内逐个删除习惯——删除时会一并清掉它的打卡记录。

English

SelfQuest collects no data. No account, no server, and the app makes no network requests. Your habits, check-ins, and focus sessions stay in the sandbox on this device.

What the app handles, and where

DataPurposeWhere
Habit names, icons, colours, repeat scheduleShow today's listApp sandbox, Application Support
Check-ins (date, XP and gold awarded)Streaks, statistics, heatmapApp sandbox
Focus sessions (task name, duration, completion)Focus statisticsApp sandbox
Character state (XP, gold, owned equipment, achievements)Level and equipment displayApp sandbox
Preferences (default focus length)Restore your last stateApp sandbox

All of it is read and written on-device and never leaves it.

Network

The app makes no network requests. Every feature works in Airplane Mode.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No collection of name, email, location, or device identifiers
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs
  • No access to Contacts, Microphone, Camera, or Health data
  • No sharing with third parties

You control your data

Settings offers two exports: JSON is a full backup you can restore on a new phone; CSV is the check-in detail for a spreadsheet. Sharing only happens when you trigger it, and the content goes through the system share sheet to a destination you pick.

Deletion

Deleting the app clears everything. You can also delete habits individually in-app, which removes their check-in history with them.
