## 2. 描述 (Description)

> 上限 4000 字符。修改需提交新版本审核。

### 简体中文

```
自律勇者把坚持做成一个有角色、有等级、有装备的游戏。

打卡和专注都会给经验与金币。经验涨等级，金币买装备，装备穿在角色身上。
没有排行榜、没有社交压力、不需要联网——你只需要对付昨天的自己。

【习惯打卡】
- 自定义名称、图标和颜色
- 三种重复方式：每天、工作日、周末
- 连续打卡有连击加成，封顶 2 倍金币
- 工作日习惯跳过周末不算断
- 暂时不想做的可以归档，历史记录保留

【番茄钟】
- 15 / 25 / 45 / 60 分钟预设
- 也支持不限时的正计时
- 完成给全额奖励，中途结束按 40% 折算
- 专注满 5 分钟才计入，避免开关刷分

【角色养成】
- 经验等级曲线，升级有反馈
- 9 件装备，分武器 / 护甲 / 饰品三个槽位
- 装备有等级门槛，攒够了才能穿
- 11 个成就，覆盖打卡、连击、专注、收集

【统计】
- 打卡热力图，一眼看到坚持的密度
- 打卡率、打卡次数、专注时长、最长连击
- 专注柱状图与习惯排行
- 支持本周 / 近 30 天 / 近 90 天

【数据是你自己的】
- 没有账号，App 也不发起任何网络请求
- 全部记录只写在这台设备的沙盒里
- 可导出 JSON 完整备份与 CSV 打卡明细
- 删除 App 即清除全部数据

【系统要求】
需要 iOS 17.0 或更高版本。iPhone 与 iPad 均可。
```

**字符数：约 555 / 4000**

### English

```
SelfQuest turns consistency into a game with a character, levels, and gear.

Check-ins and focus sessions both earn XP and gold. XP raises your level, gold buys equipment, and your hero wears it. No leaderboards, no social pressure, no network required. The only person you are competing with is yesterday's you.

[Habits]
- Custom names, icons, and colours
- Three schedules: daily, weekdays, weekends
- Streaks boost the reward per check-in, capped at double
- A weekday habit skips the weekend without breaking
- Archive a habit you are pausing, keeping its history

[Focus timer]
- Presets for 15, 25, 45, and 60 minutes
- A count-up mode with no limit
- Completing pays in full; stopping early pays 40 percent
- Five minutes minimum, so toggling it on and off earns nothing

[Character]
- An XP curve with feedback at every level
- Nine pieces of equipment across weapon, armour, and accessory slots
- Level requirements, so gear unlocks as you progress
- Eleven achievements covering streaks, focus time, and collecting

[Statistics]
- A check-in heatmap that shows the density of your effort
- Completion rate, total check-ins, focus minutes, longest streak
- Focus bar chart and a habit ranking
- Ranges for this week, 30 days, and 90 days

[Your data stays yours]
- No account, and the app makes no network requests
- Everything is stored in this device's sandbox
- Export a full JSON backup or a CSV of check-ins
- Deleting the app clears all of it

[Requirements]
iOS 17.0 or later. iPhone and iPad.
```
