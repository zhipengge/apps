中文

牧云电视不收集你的数据，也没有任何属于我们的服务器。 但它确实会联网——联网是它的全部用途。它不是靠「不联网」来保护隐私的，而是靠两件事：所有请求都发往你自己选的地址，以及发出去的东西里没有一样能指认到你。这一句是可核对的：Swift 代码里字面写死的网址一共只有五个——一键导入的那份公开表、演示频道用的 Apple 公开测试流、导入框里的示例占位符，以及关于页的两个链接（后两个是交给系统浏览器打开的，App 自己不会去请求）。除此之外，所有地址都来自你自己导入的列表。工程里零第三方依赖；没有账号、没有登录、没有后端、没有广告 / 分析 / 崩溃上报 SDK。

先说清楚这个 App 是什么

它是一个播放工具，不是内容源。 它不提供、不托管、不内置任何电视频道或节目表。你不导入任何东西，打开它就是一张空列表——这不是 bug，是设计。列表里的每一条都是你自己加的，或者是你从我们那份公开测试表里一键导入的。

主动测速：这件事不需要你点，所以单独说

列表里同一个频道常常有好几条线路。为了把能用的排在前面，App 会在列表出现的时候就对你列表里的线路各发一次探测——不是等你点了才发。你列表里有 200 条地址，就可能有两百台服务器在你的手机上看过一次连接。 这件事我们写在最前面，因为它跟「你点了才会有网络」的直觉不一样。

它的边界是明确的：最多同时 5 条、每条 6 秒超时、最多下 64 KB 就主动断开，而且刻意绕开本机缓存去测真实速度。结果只写在你自己手机上的一个键里，用来排序和自动换线。你可以在设置里关掉「按测速排序」「失败自动换线」「打开时用最快线路」，但探测本身没有单独的开关——这一条是当前版本的限制，不在设置里假装有。

我们会向源站声明什么：一个伪装成 Safari 的 User-Agent

导入、测速、播放、时移这四处，请求头里的 User-Agent 都是同一句话：

Mozilla/5.0 (iPhone; CPU iPhone OS 17_0 like Mac OS X) AppleWebKit/605.1.15 (KHTML, like Gecko) Version/17.0 Mobile/15E148 Safari/604.1

说白了：源站看到的是「一台 iPhone 上的 Safari」，看不出这是牧云电视。 这是刻意的——很多直播源会拒绝不认识客户端，不这么做大量源根本拉不动。但它是我们主动发出去的一句话，所以在这里如实写出来，而不是藏在代码里。除了这个 UA 和必要的 HTTP 头，请求里不带任何设备标识符、不给任何 ID。

明文 HTTP：为什么允许，以及这意味着什么

App 的传输安全设置里，任意加载是打开的。原因是现实：大量公开直播源本身就只提供 http://，如果严格只允许 https，列表里会有相当一部分直接播不了。

后果也要说清楚：对接明文 http 的源时，你和那台源站之间的流量是不加密的。 我们看到的是「用户自己导入的地址」，所以这个开关不该由我们替你把源换掉；能做的只是把这件事讲明白。想只在 https 下用，就只导入 https 的地址。

另外，web 内容那一项我们是关掉的——因为 App 里根本没有网页视图，没有要放行的地方。

Cookie、缓存，和「时移临时文件」

这三样都会在本机留下东西，逐条说：

  • Cookie： 播放和拉取时源站可能下发 Cookie，系统会把它们保留在 App 的容器里。代码里没有任何清理 Cookie 的逻辑。 它们只在这台设备上，但不会自己消失；删掉 App 就一起没了。
  • 缓存： App 没有自定义缓存策略，用的是系统默认的共享缓存。所以台标图片和你导入的列表内容会被写进 App 的 Caches 目录，由系统按自己的规则回收。测速那一路是刻意绕开缓存的，但只是不读，不是不写。
  • 时移临时文件： 同一个源连续播放约 1 分钟后，App 会把刚刚播过的分片暂存在系统临时目录里，让你能往回拖。上限是硬的：最多 30 分钟、400 MB、切台时最多保留 3 路。正常退出播放页时它会自己删掉。但如果你强杀 App、或者它崩溃了，那份临时目录会留在原地，直到系统回收临时文件——App 目前没有在启动时清扫残留。监控墙不缓存。

权限

本 App 不申请任何权限。 它不读取麦克风、相机、相册、通讯录、位置、健康数据或日历，不发送通知，也不申请追踪许可。App 的属性列表里没有任何权限用途描述，所以 iOS 不会为它弹出权限询问。

⚠️ 有一条本轮无法核实，需要真机确认：本地网络权限。 App 支持填局域网里的摄像头地址（比如 http://192.168.x.x/...），而 iOS 14 之后访问本地网络有独立权限。代码里没有 NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription，也没有 Bonjour 声明。静态读代码判断不出填一个局域网地址时会不会弹窗、或者会不会被系统拦下。

上架前必须在真机上填一个 http://192.168.x.x/... 试一次，据此决定是补一条用途描述，还是把这条能力从文案里去掉。在那之前，这个 App 的「零权限」只对公网地址成立。

播放行为

English

Muyun TV collects no data from you, and there is no server of ours anywhere. It does use the network: that is what it is for. It does not protect your privacy by staying offline, but by two things: every request goes to an address you chose, and nothing it sends can be tied to you. That is checkable: the Swift source contains exactly five hard-coded URLs: the public playlist behind Quick Import, the Apple test stream used by the demo channel, the example placeholder in the import field, and the two links on the About card, which are handed to the system browser rather than fetched by the app. Every other address comes from the list you imported. The project has zero third-party dependencies; there is no account, no sign-in, no backend, and no ads, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

First, what this app is

It is a player, not a content source. It does not provide, host or bundle any TV channel or programme guide. If you import nothing, you open it to an empty list, which is the design rather than a bug. Every entry in the list is either something you added or something from our public test playlist that you chose to import.

Speed testing: this happens without you tapping anything

A single channel often has several lines. To sort the working ones first, the app probes the lines in your list as soon as the list appears, not when you tap something. If your list has 200 addresses, up to two hundred servers may see one connection from your phone. This is stated up front because it does not match the intuition that nothing happens until you tap.

The limits are explicit: at most 5 at a time, a 6-second timeout each, at most 64 KB before the app disconnects, and it deliberately bypasses the local cache so the measurement is real. The results are written to one key on your own phone and used for sorting and for switching lines. You can turn off sort-by-speed, auto-switch-on-failure and prefer-fastest in Settings, but probing itself has no separate switch. That is a limitation of this version, and the app does not pretend otherwise by offering a toggle that does nothing.

What the app declares to a source: a user-agent posing as Safari

Import, speed testing, playback and timeshift all send the same user-agent:

Mozilla/5.0 (iPhone; CPU iPhone OS 17_0 like Mac OS X) AppleWebKit/605.1.15 (KHTML, like Gecko) Version/17.0 Mobile/15E148 Safari/604.1

Plainly: a source sees an iPhone running Safari, not Muyun TV. That is deliberate, because many live sources refuse clients they do not recognise, and without it a large share of them would simply not load. But it is a statement we send out, so it is written here rather than left in the code. Beyond that header and the HTTP headers the request needs, nothing goes out: no device identifier, no ID of any kind.

Plain HTTP: why it is allowed, and what it means

The app's transport security setting permits arbitrary loads. The reason is practical: many public live sources are served over http:// only, and an https-only policy would make a good share of them unplayable.

The consequence needs saying too: when a source is plain http, the traffic between you and that source is not encrypted. The addresses are ones the user imported, so this is not a switch we should be throwing for you by rewriting your sources; all we can do is be clear about it. If you want to stay on https only, import only https addresses.

The web-content half of that setting is off, because the app has no web view at all and nothing to exempt.

One clarification: the channel list is never sent to a server. That is a hard rule in the code, and the app has no upload entry point. If you export the JSON, only the destination you pick in the system share sheet ever receives it.

Cookies, caches, and the timeshift temporary files

These three leave something behind on the device, so each is spelled out:

  • Cookies: a source may set cookies during playback or fetching, and the system keeps them in the app's container. There is no cookie-clearing code anywhere. They stay on this device, but they do not disappear on their own; deleting the app removes them.
  • Caches: the app sets no custom cache policy and uses the system's default shared cache, so logo images and the bodies of playlists you import are written into the app's Caches directory, reclaimed by the system on its own schedule. The speed test deliberately bypasses the cache, but that only stops it reading, not writing.
  • Timeshift temporary files: after about a minute on the same source, the app stores the segments you just watched in the system temporary directory so you can rewind. The caps are hard: at most 30 minutes, 400 MB, and up to 3 lines retained when you switch. Leaving the player deletes it. But if you force-quit the app or it crashes, that directory stays behind until the system reclaims temporary files, because the app does not sweep leftovers at launch. The monitor wall does not cache.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not touch the microphone, camera, photo library, contacts, location, health data or calendar, it sends no notifications, and it asks for no tracking permission. Its property list declares no usage descriptions, so iOS has nothing to prompt you about.

⚠️ One item could not be verified and needs a real-device check: local network permission. The app supports LAN camera addresses (for example http://192.168.x.x/...), and since iOS 14 local network access has its own permission. The code contains no NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription and no Bonjour declaration. Reading the code cannot tell you whether entering a LAN address triggers a prompt or is silently blocked.

Before submitting, enter a http://192.168.x.x/... address on a real device and see. Then either add a usage description or drop that capability from the copy. Until then, this app's "no permissions" claim holds for public addresses only.

Playback behaviour

Playback, Picture in Picture, background audio and AirPlay all go through the system player. Two things are worth knowing:

  • Background audio is enabled (the audio background mode), so sound continues when you leave the app.
  • With AirPlay, the stream's address is handed to the receiving device (an Apple TV, say), which fetches it. That is system behaviour rather than the app sending content to us, and there is no "us" to send it to.

Export, sharing and screenshots

Exporting the playlist as JSON, capturing a frame, and sharing all happen only when you press the button, and all go through the system share sheet, where the destination is your choice; the app neither knows nor records which app you picked. The generated files start in the app's temporary directory (muyuntv_channels.json, muyuntv-NAME.png) and are reclaimed under the system's rules for temporary files. Screenshots are not written to the photo library and need no photo permission.

The disclaimer and the report path

The app contains a disclaimer and a report entry point. Reporting sends an email to gezhipeng0201@163.com through the system mail client; the app itself does not go online to do it. That public playlist and that entry point exist for the same reason: this app does not pretend to be a content platform.

What we do not do

  • No upload of your channel list, and no feature that would upload it
  • No ad networks, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs (the app contains no third-party code at all, and the project has no package dependencies)
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location
  • No recording, no face detection, and no uploading of monitor-wall video
  • No sign-in and no account
  • No GameKit and no leaderboards
  • No bundled channels or programme guide
  • No system logging

Deletion

  • Swipe a single channel to delete it; Settings can clear the whole list, and a cleared list is never refilled.
  • Monitor-wall pins, favourites, recents and playback statistics can be cleared in Settings, or along with the list.
  • Leaving the player deletes the timeshift temporary files (force-quit caveat above).
  • Deleting the app clears all of the above, including that temporary directory and the cookies in the container. Export the JSON first if you want to keep the list.

Children and content

The app collects no one's data, children included. It has no accounts, no chat and no user-generated content. One caveat: the content is entirely whatever you import. The app does not filter, review or supply any channel, so whether it suits a child depends on the sources in your list. There is no built-in browser and no web view.
