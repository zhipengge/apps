中文

速闻不收集你的阅读记录，不需要账号。 没有自建后端，不内嵌广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

我们处理什么、在哪处理

数据用途位置
已缓存的新闻内容离线阅读与快速打开设备本地
收藏的新闻条目跨期保留你标记的内容设备本地
外观等偏好设置恢复上次的使用状态设备本地

我们不记录你读过什么。 应用不会上传阅读历史，也没有可用于识别你的标识符。

网络通信

应用从公开的 GitHub 仓库拉取每日新闻内容（JSON 文件）。请求中不包含你的个人信息、阅读记录或收藏列表。图片由内容中标注的公开图源提供。

你可以离线阅读已缓存的期次。

分享

分享功能完全由你主动触发。生成分享文稿后，内容通过系统分享面板交给你选择的目标应用。我们不参与也不记录这个过程。

我们不做的事

  • 不要求注册或登录
  • 不记录或上传阅读历史
  • 不接入广告、分析或统计服务
  • 不读取通讯录、位置、麦克风、摄像头
  • 不把你的数据共享给第三方

数据删除

删除 App 即可清除全部本地数据，包括缓存与收藏。若要保留收藏，请先导出备份。

English

Speed does not track what you read and needs no account. No backend of our own, no ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

What we process, and where

DataPurposeWhere
Cached article contentOffline reading and fast openingOn device
Bookmarked articlesKeep saved items across issuesOn device
Appearance preferencesRestore your last stateOn device

We do not record what you read. Reading history is never uploaded, and there is no identifier we could attach it to.

Network

The app fetches daily content (JSON files) from a public GitHub repository. Those requests carry no personal information, reading history, or bookmarks. Images come from the public sources cited in each article.

Cached issues can be read offline.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you trigger it. The generated text is handed to the system share sheet and you choose the destination. We are not involved and keep no record.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No recording or uploading of reading history
  • No ads, analytics, or statistics services
  • No access to Contacts, Location, Microphone, or Camera
  • No data sharing with third parties

Deletion

Deleting the app clears all local data, including cache and bookmarks. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.
