隐私政策（Privacy Policy）

最后更新日期：2026-01-01

Gwave 非常重视用户的隐私。本应用是一款网络电台与音频播放工具，旨在为用户提供简单、纯粹的声音播放体验。

一、我们收集的信息

Gwave 不会收集、存储或共享任何可用于识别用户身份的个人信息。

具体而言：

无需注册或登录账号

不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、位置等个人数据

不收集用户的音频内容或播放记录

应用仅会在系统层面处理必要的技术信息，以确保正常运行，例如由 iOS 系统自动提供的基础运行状态信息。

二、音频播放与网络请求

Gwave 支持播放内置电台以及用户自行添加的网络电台。
所有音频内容均 直接从对应的网络音频源传输到用户设备，Gwave 不会对音频内容进行存储、分析或再分发。

三、第三方服务

Gwave 不使用任何第三方广告、分析、统计或跟踪服务，也不会将任何用户数据共享给第三方。

四、儿童隐私

Gwave 不会主动收集 13 岁以下儿童的任何个人信息。如您认为有儿童在使用本应用并提供了个人信息，请通过 App Store 联系我们，我们将及时处理。

五、隐私政策的变更

我们可能会根据功能调整或法律要求更新本隐私政策。
任何变更都会在本页面中公布，并更新“最后更新日期”。

六、联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问或建议，可通过 App Store 中提供的开发者联系方式与我们取得联系。

Privacy Policy (English Version)

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Gwave respects your privacy. This app is designed as a simple internet radio and audio playback tool, providing users with a clean and focused listening experience.

1. Information We Collect

Gwave does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

Specifically:

No account registration or login is required

No collection of names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data

No collection of audio content or listening history

Only basic system-level technical information provided automatically by iOS may be processed to ensure normal app functionality.

2. Audio Streaming and Network Access

Gwave allows users to play built-in radio stations and user-added internet radio streams.
All audio streams are delivered directly from the selected source to the user’s device. Gwave does not store, analyze, or redistribute audio content.

3. Third-Party Services

Gwave does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or data collection services, and does not share user data with any third parties.

4. Children’s Privacy

Gwave does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
If you believe that a child has provided personal information while using this app, please contact us through the App Store developer contact information.

5. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect functional or legal changes.
Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the developer contact details available on the App Store.
