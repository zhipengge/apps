中文

怎么玩？

按住屏幕任意位置拖动，机体跟着你的手指走，机炮自动开火。你不需要瞄准，只需要躲子弹、决定打谁。竖屏，单手就能玩。

障碍是干什么用的？

可破坏的障碍会同时挡住你和敌人的子弹，而道具常常就藏在障碍后面。继续打敌人还是分火力砸开障碍，是每一波都要做的取舍。障碍有血量，把它打碎，藏在里面的东西才会掉出来，但并非每个障碍都有货。

星级怎么算？

只看两件事：走到第几波、总共挨了几下。本作是自动开火，玩家没有瞄准这个动作，所以命中率衡量不了水平。走得远、挨得少就是 3 星。中途阵亡的局也有星，不只是通关才给。

完美波次是什么？

一波敌人一个都没漏掉，就是完美波次，回复 1 点生命。已经满血时也照样算完美波次，只是没有血可回。机体初始 5 点生命。

机体怎么解锁？

隼默认可用；扇需要 4 颗星，芒 12 颗，寻 24 颗。星数只用来解锁机体，不卖数值，也没有抽卡。四种机体的弹道和射速都不一样：隼射速快，扇是三向扇形，芒是穿透激光，寻自动追踪最近的敌人。

免费版能玩到什么程度？

战役可以玩到第 8 波，够玩懂自动开火、拖拽走位、障碍取舍和机体差异。完整版一次买断，解锁战役全部 40 波、无尽模式与 Boss Rush。没有广告，没有体力，没有订阅。

换了手机，购买会丢吗？

不会。用同一个 Apple ID，在设置页点「恢复购买」即可，不需要重新付费。

数据存在哪里？怎么删？

全部只在这台设备上，不上传也不同步。设置页有「重置全部进度」，会清空星数、金币、最高分和已解锁的机体；删除 App 即清除全部数据。详见隐私政策。

战绩卡怎么发出去？

结算页点「分享战绩」，App 在本机把这一局渲染成一张竖版图片（分数、星级、击杀、受伤次数、存活时间、金币），交给系统分享面板，你选微信或任何装了的应用。

有声音吗？

有，音效由代码实时合成，跟随系统静音键，也不会打断你在听的音乐。设置里可以关。工程里没有任何音频文件。

需要联网吗？

不需要。飞行模式下全部功能可用。

怎么反馈问题？

到 GitHub Issues 提交，附上设备型号、系统版本和复现步骤。

English

How do I play

Hold anywhere and drag; your ship follows your finger and the guns fire on their own. There is no aiming to do, only dodging and choosing what to shoot. Portrait, one hand.

What are the barriers for

Destructible barriers stop both your bullets and the enemy's, and power-ups often hide behind them. Keep shooting enemies or divert fire to break a barrier: that trade-off comes up every wave. Barriers have health, and only give up what they hide when destroyed, though not every barrier hides anything.

How is the star rating decided

By two things only: how far you got and how many hits you took. The guns fire automatically, so accuracy cannot say anything about how you played. Go far and take few hits for three stars. A run that ends in death still earns stars; they are not reserved for clears.

What is a perfect wave

Clear a wave without letting a single enemy escape and you restore one point of health. It still counts as perfect at full health, where there is nothing to restore. Ships start with five health.

How do I unlock the ships

The Falcon is available from the start; the Spreader needs 4 stars, the Laser 12, and the Homing 24. Stars only unlock ships. Nothing is sold for power and there is no gacha. Each ship fires differently: fast straight shots, a three-way spread, a piercing laser, and homing shots that chase the nearest enemy.

What does the free version include

Campaign waves up to wave 8, which is enough to learn the auto-fire, the drag controls, the barrier trade-off, and the differences between ships. One purchase unlocks all 40 campaign waves, Endless, and Boss Rush. No ads, no energy, no subscription.

Do I lose my purchase on a new phone

No. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore Purchases in Settings. You will not be charged twice.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it

On this device only; nothing is uploaded or synced. Settings offers Reset All Progress, which clears stars, coins, best scores, and unlocked ships. Deleting the app clears everything. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I share a run

Tap Share on the result screen. The app renders the run into a portrait image on-device (score, stars, kills, hits taken, survival time, coins) and hands it to the system share sheet, where you pick any app you have installed.

Is there sound

Yes. Sound effects are synthesised in code at play time, respect the silent switch, and do not interrupt music you are listening to. They can be turned off in Settings. The app ships no audio files.

Does it need a network connection

No. Every feature works in Airplane Mode.

Reporting a problem

File an issue at GitHub Issues with your device model, OS version, and steps to reproduce.
