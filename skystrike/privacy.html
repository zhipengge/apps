空中绞杀 · 隐私政策

SkyStrike — Privacy Policy · 最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-10

中文

空中绞杀不收集任何数据。 没有账号，没有服务器，App 也不发起任何网络请求。你的星数、金币和每一局的记录只写在这台设备的沙盒里。

我们处理什么、存在哪

数据用途位置
存档：累计星数、金币、各模式的最高分、最远波数、通关状态记录进度、解锁机体设备沙盒 UserDefaults
累计数据：总击杀数、总游玩局数、总时长设置页的统计展示设备沙盒
上次选择的机体、音效开关恢复上次的使用状态设备沙盒
一局的结算数据：分数、星级、击杀、受伤次数、存活时间结算页展示、生成战绩卡图片仅本机内存；只有你点「分享战绩」时才渲染成图片

以上数据全部在本机读写，不会离开设备。本 App 不使用 iCloud 或任何云同步。

网络

App 不发起任何网络请求。 飞行模式下全部功能可用，包括内购的恢复购买（由系统商店进程处理）。

完整版购买

完整版是一次性内购，交易全程由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。App 只向系统查询「这个 Apple ID 是否已购买」，拿不到也存不下你的支付方式、账单信息或 Apple ID。购买记录保存在 Apple 那边，所以换设备后用「恢复购买」就能找回，不需要在本 App 里注册任何账号。

我们不做的事

  • 不要求注册或登录
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、位置、设备标识符
  • 不接入广告、分析或追踪 SDK
  • 不把存档上传或同步到任何服务器
  • 不读取通讯录、相册、麦克风、摄像头、位置
  • 不把你的数据共享给任何第三方

分享

分享完全由你主动触发。点「分享战绩」时，App 在本机把这一局的结果渲染成一张图片，交给系统分享面板，由你选择发给谁。App 不知道也不记录你选了哪个应用。

数据删除

设置页有「重置全部进度」，会清空星数、金币、最高分与已解锁的机体。删除 App 即清除全部数据。

English

SkyStrike collects no data. No account, no server, and the app makes no network requests. Your stars, coins, and run history live only in the sandbox on this device.

What the app handles, and where

DataPurposeWhere
Save data: total stars, coins, best score and furthest wave per mode, campaign clearTrack progress and unlock shipsApp sandbox, UserDefaults
Totals: kills, runs played, time playedShown on the Settings screenApp sandbox
Last ship selected, sound toggleRestore your last stateApp sandbox
Per-run results: score, stars, kills, hits taken, survival timeResult screen and the score card imageIn memory only; rendered into an image when you tap Share

All of it is read and written on-device and never leaves it. There is no iCloud or cloud sync of any kind.

Network

The app makes no network requests. Every feature works in Airplane Mode, including Restore Purchases, which the system store process handles.

The full version purchase

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app only asks the system whether this Apple ID already owns it, and never sees or stores your payment method, billing details, or Apple ID. The purchase record lives with Apple, so Restore Purchases brings it back on a new device with no account to create here.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No collection of name, email, location, or device identifiers
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs
  • No upload or sync of your save data
  • No access to Contacts, Photos, Microphone, Camera, or Location
  • No sharing with third parties

Sharing

Sharing only happens when you trigger it. When you tap Share, the app renders that run into an image on-device and hands it to the system share sheet, where you pick the destination. The app neither knows nor records which app you chose.

Deletion

Settings offers Reset All Progress, which clears stars, coins, best scores, and unlocked ships. Deleting the app clears everything.
