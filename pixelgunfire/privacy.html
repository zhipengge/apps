像素枪火 · 隐私政策

PixelGunfire — Privacy Policy · 最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-12

中文

像素枪火不收集任何数据。 没有账号，没有服务器，App 不发起任何网络请求。「不联网」在这里是一句可核对的话：App 的 20 个 Swift 文件只 import 了 Foundation、SwiftUI、UIKit、AVFoundation、Combine、QuartzCore 六个系统框架，没有链接任何网络库；工程里没有 SPM 包、没有 Podfile，框架构建阶段是空的；构建产物里唯一的网址是 Apple 自己写进每个二进制的属性列表 DTD 标识符，装载真实代码的那份 dylib 里一个网址都没有。你的进度存在设备上的三个偏好设置键里，不上传也不同步。

我们处理什么、存在哪

数据用途位置
关卡进度：每一关的最好评级（S / A / B / C）、最好分数、是否通关、通关次数让你下次打开时进度还在；成绩菜单读的就是它本机偏好设置，键名 pixelgunfire.progress.v1
触感开关：开或关记住你的设置本机偏好设置，键名 pixelgunfire.haptics.v1
音效开关：开或关记住你的设置本机偏好设置，键名 pixelgunfire.sound.v1

就这三个键，没有别的。以上数据全部在本机读写，不会离开设备。本 App 不使用 iCloud，也不使用任何云同步。

这些内容里没有任何可以指认到人的东西：没有姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、设备标识符或广告标识符，也没有账号 ID——因为这个 App 根本没有账号。它也没有广告标识符读取代码，没有把任何东西送给第三方。

没有文件，也没有数据库

除了上面三个键，工程里没有 FileManager 的任何调用，没有 SwiftData、CoreData、Keychain、iCloud、CloudKit。App 不往磁盘上写任何文件，也没有装应用数据的地方——一切持久化就是这三个键。

网络

App 不发起任何网络请求。 飞行模式下全部功能可用，包括第一次启动。8 个关卡、4 把武器、敌人和 boss 的全部数据都写死在代码里随 App 打包，没有任何一处需要下载。App 里没有排行榜、没有成就上报、没有云存档。

购买与账号

本 App 没有内购，没有账号系统，不需要注册或登录。 代码里没有任何支付相关的接口，App 内没有付费入口，也没有解锁码或免费版和完整版的区分。8 个关卡从第一次打开起就全部可以打。如果你是通过 App Store 取得本 App 的，那笔交易由 Apple 处理：我们不接触你的支付信息，也拿不到你的 Apple ID。

权限

本 App 不申请任何权限。 它不读取麦克风、相机、相册、通讯录、位置、健康数据或日历，不发送通知，也不申请任何追踪许可。App 的属性列表里没有任何权限用途描述，所以 iOS 根本不会为它弹出权限询问。

声音

音效全部是运行时用代码合成的，App 里没有一个音频文件。 音频会话的模式是环境音，并且明确打开了「与其他音频混合」，所以它不会打断你正在听的音乐，也不会在后台继续占用音频。它不录音，也不使用麦克风。

触感

受击、通关这类事件会让你手上震一下，由系统的触感引擎产生。它只是给手指的反馈，不产生也不读取任何数据。震动是有节流的，而且开火和换武器这类高频动作被特意设计成不震，不会震个不停。

不会离开设备的按钮

这个 App 里没有分享、没有导出、没有剪贴板操作，也没有任何一个能把数据送出去的按钮。也就是说，除了你自己看屏幕，数据没有任何出口——不是「我们不主动发」，是根本没有这条路径。

设备备份

说白了：那三个键就在 App 的偏好设置里。如果你在系统里开了 iCloud 备份或电脑备份，那份备份会包含它们，和它包含你其他 App 的数据一样。那份备份由 Apple 保管，我们碰不到它；本 App 自己不会把任何东西传到任何地方。

我们不做的事

  • 不要求注册或登录
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、位置、设备标识符或广告标识符
  • 不接入广告、分析、崩溃上报或追踪 SDK（本 App 不含任何第三方代码，工程里没有任何依赖包）
  • 不录音，不读取麦克风、相册、通讯录、位置
  • 不发送通知，也不申请任何系统权限
  • 不把你的数据共享给任何第三方

数据删除

设置里有「清空战绩」的入口，点下去会清掉前面那张表里的第一条（关卡进度），触感和音效这两个开关会保留——因为你调的是偏好，不是进度。 想彻底清空就删除 App，三个键会一起消失。

儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的数据，当然也不收集儿童的数据。它没有账号、没有聊天、没有用户生成内容，也没有任何形式的联网功能。游戏内容是像素风格的虚构战斗，不涉及任何真实人物或真实事件。

English

PixelGunfire collects no data. No account, no server, and the app makes no network requests. That claim is checkable: the app's 20 Swift files import six system frameworks only (Foundation, SwiftUI, UIKit, AVFoundation, Combine and QuartzCore), no networking library is linked, the project has no Swift packages and no Podfile and its frameworks build phase is empty, and the only URL in the built app is the property-list DTD identifier Apple compiles into every binary; the dylib that holds the real code contains no URL at all. Your progress sits in three preference keys on this device and is never uploaded or synced.

What the app handles, and where

DataPurposeWhere
Level progress: your best rating on each level (S, A, B or C), best score, whether you cleared it, and how many timesSo your progress is still there next time, and so the scores menu has something to readOn-device preferences, key pixelgunfire.progress.v1
Haptics on or offTo remember your settingOn-device preferences, key pixelgunfire.haptics.v1
Sound on or offTo remember your settingOn-device preferences, key pixelgunfire.sound.v1

Those three keys and nothing else. All of it is read and written on-device and never leaves it. There is no iCloud and no cloud sync of any kind.

None of it can be tied to a person: no name, email, phone number, location, device identifier or advertising identifier, and no account ID, because the app has no accounts. It never reads an advertising identifier and never hands anything to a third party.

No files, and no database

Beyond those three keys the project makes no use of FileManager at all, and contains no SwiftData, CoreData, Keychain, iCloud or CloudKit. The app writes no files to disk and has nowhere to put application data. All persistence is the three keys above.

Network

The app makes no network requests. Every feature works in Airplane Mode, including first launch. All eight levels, all four weapons, every enemy and the boss are defined in code and ship inside the app; nothing is ever downloaded. There is no leaderboard, no achievement reporting and no cloud save.

Purchases and accounts

The app has no in-app purchases, no account system, and nothing to register or sign in to. There is no payment code of any kind, no purchase screen, no unlock code and no free-versus-full split. All eight levels can be played from the first launch. If you obtained the app through the App Store, that transaction was handled by Apple: we never see your payment details and we do not receive your Apple ID.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not touch the microphone, camera, photo library, contacts, location, health data or calendar, it sends no notifications, and it asks for no tracking permission. The app's property list declares no usage descriptions, so iOS has nothing to prompt you about.

Sound

Every sound effect is synthesized in code at runtime; the app contains no audio files. The audio session runs in ambient mode with mixing with other audio explicitly enabled, so it will not interrupt music you are listening to and it holds no background audio. It does not record and does not use the microphone.

Haptics

Events like taking a hit or clearing a level give your hand a tap, produced by the system's haptic engine. It is feedback for your hand and neither produces nor reads any data. Taps are throttled, and high-frequency actions like firing and switching weapons are deliberately left silent rather than buzzing non-stop.

The button that sends data nowhere

This app has no sharing, no export and no clipboard access, and no button that could send anything out. Apart from you looking at the screen, the data has no exit at all: it is not that we choose not to send it, there is no path.

Device backups

Plainly: those three keys live in the app's preferences. If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups enabled, that backup includes them, the same way it includes data from your other apps. That backup is held by Apple and we cannot reach it; the app itself sends nothing anywhere.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No collection of name, email, location, device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs (the app contains no third-party code at all, and the project has no package dependencies)
  • No recording and no microphone, photos, contacts or location access
  • No notifications, and no permission requests of any kind
  • No sharing with third parties

Deletion

Settings has a reset-progress entry point. It clears the first row of the table above (level progress) and deliberately keeps the haptics and sound toggles, because those are preferences rather than progress. To clear everything, delete the app: all three keys go with it.

Children

The app collects no one's data, children included. It has no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, and no networking of any kind. The game is pixel-art fictional combat and refers to no real person or event.
