首次发布。竖屏单机跑轰，一个拇指就能玩。左下角是一个拨杆，四个方向各管一件事：左右拨是跑，上拨是跳、按住不放跳得更高，下拨是滑铲且前段有无敌帧，斜着拨可以边跑边跳；右下角还有单独一个滑铲按钮。没有开火键，也没有瞄准，枪会自动锁定射程内的目标并自动开火。8 个手工设计的关卡，一关大概 6 分钟。挨一下就掉一条命，没有血条，每关 5 条命。7 种敌人，外加一个三阶段的 boss。打完按时间、存活和冲锋槽三项加权给出 S 到 C 的评级。结算页会把你的死亡按原因归类，针对最主要的那一条给一条具体建议。也可以让机器人替你打一局，它不记成绩。所有画面由代码实时绘制，所有音效由代码实时合成，仓库里没有一张图片素材、没有一个音频文件。全程离线，没有账号，没有广告，没有内购，不申请任何系统权限。

---

First release. A portrait single-player run-and-gun you play with one thumb. One stick in the bottom-left does four jobs: left and right to run, up to jump with the hold setting the height, down to slide, whose opening frames are invincible, and diagonally to run and jump at once. There is a separate slide button in the bottom-right as well. There is no fire button and no aiming, because the gun acquires targets in range and fires by itself. Eight hand-designed levels, about six minutes each. One hit costs one life, there is no health bar, and you get five lives per level. Seven enemy types plus a three-phase boss. When a level ends you get an S to C rating from time, survival and the charge gauge. The result screen groups your deaths by cause and gives one concrete piece of advice for the biggest one. A bot can also play a level for you, and demo runs do not record scores. Every visual is drawn at runtime and every sound is synthesized at runtime; the project contains no image assets and no audio files. Fully offline: no account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no permission prompts.
