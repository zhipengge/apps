首次发布。俯视角单机战术射击，10 个关卡，一枪就能把你打死：玩家身上没有血量这个字段，被打中就是死，唯一的缓冲是敌人发现你之后举枪的那一下，最快 0.40 秒，最慢 0.80 秒。左摇杆走路，右摇杆瞄准并开枪，摇杆上画着一圈虚线，圈内只瞄准，推过去才开火。右下角五个按钮：疾跑、换弹、侦察、换枪、刀。侦察会把世界放慢，但真实时间照走，九秒配额按真实秒扣，换来的是每个敌人的视野锥和巡逻路线。掩体既挡视线也挡子弹，木箱可以被打碎，水泥墙打不碎。目标有三种，不必清场。三颗星是三条独立的线：通关、用时不超过基准、被发现次数不超过基准且命中率不低于 40%。十张固定地图，同一关重打多少次都是那张图。全程离线，没有账号，没有广告，没有内购，不申请任何系统权限。

---

First release. A top-down single-player tactical shooter with ten levels where one bullet kills you: the player has no health field at all, and the only buffer is the moment an enemy raises their gun after spotting you, from 0.40 seconds at the fastest to 0.80 at the slowest. The left stick moves and the right stick aims and fires, with a dashed circle drawn on it: inside the circle you only aim, and pushing past it fires. Five buttons sit in the lower right: sprint, reload, scout, swap weapon and knife. Scouting slows the world down while real time keeps running, with a nine-second budget charged in real seconds, and what you buy is every enemy's vision cone and patrol route. Cover blocks sightlines and bullets alike, wooden crates can be shot apart and concrete walls cannot. There are three objectives and you do not have to clear the map. Three stars sit on three independent lines: clearing the level, finishing within par time, and staying within the alert budget with accuracy at or above 40 percent. Ten fixed maps, so replaying a level always gives you the same terrain. Fully offline: no account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no permission prompts.
